How to Think Like a SOC Analyst

Step 1: Observe unusual traffic patterns or unexpected spikes in packet volume.

Step 2: Identify the source and destination IP addresses.

Step 3: Establish whether the protocol and ports involved match normal operational baselines.

Step 4: Check benign causes (software updates, backup jobs, cloud sync) before escalating.

"An anomaly is a starting point for investigation, not proof of compromise."
